# Rescue Squad (beta)

Rescue Squad lets trained community volunteers respond to medical emergencies near them. This TestFlight build is
a **demonstration**: all data is synthetic, it stays on your phone, and the build has no server.

**It is not an emergency service and cannot summon help.** In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

## What to try

- Request help with a two-second hold. It opens your phone's dialer for 102, the official emergency number in
  Nepal; you place the call yourself.
- Go on call as a volunteer responder, then accept, travel to and resolve a request.
- Read sourced first-aid guides, browse AED locations and add one, and find volunteers by municipality.
- Record a voice note for the responder and take a wellbeing self-check.
- Switch between English and Nepali.

Please use made-up details only.
