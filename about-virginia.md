# Rescue Squad (US test build)

Rescue Squad lets trained community volunteers respond to medical emergencies near them. This TestFlight build is a
**US test build for invited testers in Virginia**: the emergencies are pretend, but your location is real and is sent
to a Rescue Squad test server.

**It is not an emergency service and never calls 911 for you.** For a real emergency, call 911 yourself. Do not enter
real health information about anyone.

## What to try

- Sign up with your phone number.
- Ask for help with a two-second hold, with or without an account.
- Go on call as a volunteer responder, then accept, navigate to and resolve another tester's practice request, and
  call them in the app.
- Record the patient's condition with made-up values, and read sourced first-aid guides.
