# Rescue Squad beta: privacy statement

*Applies to: the Rescue Squad **demonstration** build distributed through Apple TestFlight. Last updated:
[DATE].*

**This beta is a demonstration. It is not an emergency service and cannot summon help.** In an emergency, call
your local emergency number.

## What this beta collects

**Nothing.** The beta has no server. Everything you enter (a name, a phone number, a municipality, a role, any
practice requests, notes or voice memos) stays on your phone and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Please enter **made-up details only**: a made-up name, a made-up number and a made-up complaint. Do not enter real
personal or health information, including your own.

## What leaves your phone

Only what you choose to send yourself:

- **Feedback email.** If you tap Send feedback, your mail app opens a message to us. It includes the app version,
  your phone's OS version and model, and the build type. It does not include your name, phone number or anything
  you entered in the app. You can read and edit the message before sending it.
- **TestFlight feedback.** Screenshots and comments you send through TestFlight go to the developer through Apple.
  Apple's own privacy policy covers that channel.
- **Phone calls.** The Call 102 button opens your phone's dialer. The app does not place the call or record it.

## Location

This beta never asks for your location.

## Third parties

The beta contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting software and shares nothing with third parties.

## Deleting your data

Open **Settings → Delete your account** to erase everything the beta stored on your phone. Deleting the app also
removes it.
